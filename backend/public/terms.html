Your account

Keep your sign-in details private. One account is for one student. You're responsible for activity on your account.

Fair use of exams

  • Each exam has a server-controlled timer. When time runs out, your saved answers are submitted automatically.
  • An exam can be open in only one tab or device at a time.
  • Don't copy, record, share or redistribute questions or solutions.
  • Exam integrity events are recorded and may be reviewed by administrators. Where tests use strict mode, exceeding the stated limit submits your paper automatically. An administrator may invalidate an attempt after reviewing the evidence; this removes it from rankings.

Paid access

Paid test series unlock specific seasons of mock tests once payment is verified. Refunds follow the ACEIIIT refund policy. Access may be revoked if a payment is reversed.

Results and interpretations

Scores, ranks and percentiles are calculated by the portal from the attempts in each test. Ranks change as more students take a test. AI-assisted interpretations are guidance only and never change your scores.

Availability

We work to keep the portal available but can't guarantee uninterrupted service. Your answers are saved to the server as you work so you can recover from connection problems.
