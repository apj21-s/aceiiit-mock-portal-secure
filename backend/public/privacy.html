Privacy Policy

ACEIIIT Mock Portal · Last updated 15 October 2026

What we collect

  • Account: your name, email address, and how you sign in (password, Google or Apple). Passwords are stored only as salted hashes.
  • Exams: your answers, time per question, scores and results for each attempt.
  • Exam integrity records: during an exam we record events such as leaving the exam tab or full screen, copy/paste attempts, blocked keyboard shortcuts, and switching devices. These are shown to exam administrators for review. Nothing is decided automatically about your result from them.
  • Payments and access: which test series you have access to and payment verification status (we don't store card or bank details).
  • ACEIIIT Mentor: the messages you send to the mentor and its replies, and how many mentor replies you have used each month.
  • Planner: study reminders you create, and, if you connect Google Calendar, an encrypted access token used only to add those reminders to your calendar.
  • Technical: request logs (with secrets removed) used to keep the service secure and working.

Google user data

If you use Google with the portal, this is exactly what we receive from Google and what happens to it.

  • Sign in with Google: your name, email address and Google account ID. We use them only to create your portal account and sign you in.
  • Google Drive (portal administrators only): an administrator connects the Google Drive that holds the study material. We store that account's encrypted refresh token and email, and only read and change files inside the study-materials folder. Students' Google accounts are never connected to Drive.
  • Google Calendar (only if you connect it): permission to add your study reminders to your calendar as events, and to update or remove those events when you change or cancel a reminder. We store an encrypted refresh token, your Google account ID and the IDs of the events we create. We don't read your other calendar events.

We use Google user data only to provide these features. We don't use it for advertising and we don't sell it. It is shared only with the service providers that run the portal for us (hosting, database and email delivery, listed under Sharing), for example to send you the reminder emails you ask for. Our use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion of Google user data:

  • We keep it only while your account is active.
  • When you disconnect Google Calendar, we revoke the portal's access with Google and delete the stored token immediately.
  • When you delete your account, we revoke Google Calendar access and delete its stored data immediately. Your Google account ID, name and email are permanently deleted automatically 30 days later, together with the rest of your account.
  • Reminder events already added to your own Google Calendar stay there; you can delete them in Google Calendar.
  • You can also remove the portal's access at any time from your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions, or email us to have your data deleted.

AI-assisted performance interpretation

After an exam, the portal calculates your score, accuracy, rank and timing itself. It may then use an external AI service (Google Gemini) to write a plain-language interpretation of that structured performance evidence. AI does not calculate or alter your scores. We send only aggregate evidence such as accuracy per topic and timing patterns, never your name, email, account identifiers or the question text. On some service tiers, the provider may use submitted content to improve its products, which is why we send no personal information. If the AI service is unavailable, a rule-based interpretation is shown instead.

ACEIIIT Mentor (AI chat)

ACEIIIT Mentor answers your questions using Google Gemini. For each message we send Gemini your message and the recent messages in that conversation. When you ask about your own performance, we also send the results the portal measured for you: scores, accuracy, timing, rank and percentile of your recent mocks, and topic-level evidence. When you ask about your mistakes, we include up to five questions you answered wrongly (shortened), with the options and your choice. We never send your name, email or account identifiers. Anything you type yourself is sent as written, so please don't put personal details in your messages. On some service tiers, the provider may use submitted content to improve its products. Replies are written by AI and can be wrong; your scores are always calculated by AceIIIT, never by the AI.

Your free monthly allowance, and any larger allowance from a programme you enrolled in, is decided on our server from verified enrolment records. To check enrolment, the portal looks up your email address on the AceIIIT website (our own service).

How we use your data

To run your account and exams, compute results and rankings, provide access you've paid for, send emails you request (activation, password reset, reminders, payment confirmation), keep exams fair, and secure the service.

Sharing

We use service providers to operate the portal: hosting and database, email delivery (Resend/Brevo), image storage (Cloudinary), Google (sign-in, Calendar if you connect it, Drive for study material, and Gemini as described above), and the AceIIIT website for programme enrolment checks. We don't sell your data.

Retention

We keep your account and exam history while your account is active. AI interpretations expire after one year. ACEIIIT Mentor conversations are kept until you delete them (in the mentor, under "Your conversations") or your account is deleted.

When you delete your account, you are signed out everywhere and Google Calendar access is revoked straight away. Your account then stays closed for 30 days, after which it is permanently deleted automatically: your name, email, Google account ID, reminders, calendar data and ACEIIIT Mentor conversations are removed. Your exam history is kept only in anonymized form (without your name, email or account identifiers) so that rankings for other students stay correct. Payment records, including the email used for a purchase, are kept as required for accounting.

Your choices and rights

  • Download your data: Account → Security → "Download my data".
  • Delete your account: Account → Security → "Delete my account". It is permanently deleted 30 days later, as described under Retention. If you can't sign in, email us and we'll delete it for you.
  • Sign out everywhere: Account → Security → "Sign out of all devices".
  • Disconnect Google Calendar at any time from the dashboard; we revoke the access with Google and delete the stored token.
