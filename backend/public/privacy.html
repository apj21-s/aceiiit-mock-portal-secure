Privacy Policy

ACEIIIT Mock Portal · Last updated 7 October 2026

What we collect

  • Account: your name, email address, and how you sign in (password, Google or Apple). Passwords are stored only as salted hashes.
  • Exams: your answers, time per question, scores and results for each attempt.
  • Exam integrity records: during an exam we record events such as leaving the exam tab or full screen, copy/paste attempts, blocked keyboard shortcuts, and switching devices. These are shown to exam administrators for review. Nothing is decided automatically about your result from them.
  • Payments and access: which test series you have access to and payment verification status (we don't store card or bank details).
  • Planner: study reminders you create, and, if you connect Google Calendar, an encrypted access token used only to add those reminders to your calendar.
  • Technical: request logs (with secrets removed) used to keep the service secure and working.

AI-assisted performance interpretation

After an exam, the portal calculates your score, accuracy, rank and timing itself. It may then use an external AI service (Google Gemini) to write a plain-language interpretation of that structured performance evidence. AI does not calculate or alter your scores. We send only aggregate evidence such as accuracy per topic and timing patterns, never your name, email, account identifiers or the question text. On some service tiers, the provider may use submitted content to improve its products, which is why we send no personal information. If the AI service is unavailable, a rule-based interpretation is shown instead.

How we use your data

To run your account and exams, compute results and rankings, provide access you've paid for, send emails you request (activation, password reset, reminders, payment confirmation), keep exams fair, and secure the service.

Sharing

We use service providers to operate the portal: hosting and database, email delivery (Resend/Brevo), image storage (Cloudinary), Google (sign-in, Calendar if you connect it, and Gemini as described above). We don't sell your data.

Retention

We keep your account and exam history while your account is active. AI interpretations expire after one year. When you delete your account, you are signed out everywhere, and your exam history is anonymized when the deletion is processed.

Your choices and rights

  • Download your data: Account → Security → "Download my data".
  • Delete your account: Account → Security → "Delete my account".
  • Sign out everywhere: Account → Security → "Sign out of all devices".
  • Disconnect Google Calendar at any time from the dashboard; we revoke the access token with Google.

Questions or requests: support@aceiiit.com
